Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

This policy covers Chad, an AI fitness accountability coach delivered by text message at +1 (660) 243-3454. Chad is operated by Tyler Steele, a sole proprietor ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The text messages you send to Chad and the messages Chad sends you.
  • Information you choose to share in those messages, such as fitness goals, workout schedule, workouts completed, and check-in responses.
  • Your opt-in and opt-out history, including when you replied YES, STOP, or START.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and send you check-ins about your own goals and progress.
  • To remember your goals and workouts so Chad's messages are relevant to you.
  • To keep a record of your consent and honor opt-out requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not send marketing messages.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers below that are needed to run the service.

To operate Chad we use two service providers:

  • Twilio, which carries text messages between your phone and Chad.
  • Anthropic, whose AI model generates Chad's replies. Your messages and the stored details about your goals and workouts are sent to Anthropic's API to produce each reply.

These providers process data only to deliver the service. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and fitness information while you're using Chad. If you text STOP, Chad stops messaging you. To have your data deleted, email us and we will delete it, except for a minimal record of your opt-out, which we keep so we don't message you again.

Security

Data is stored on systems we control and access is limited to the operator. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Chad is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: tsteele842@gmail.com. You can also reply HELP to any message.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
